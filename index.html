Natal is a boutique residential complex in La Veleta, one of Tulum's most walkable neighborhoods. The condo is set up for short stays and longer work-from-Tulum visits alike: a full kitchen, in-unit washer/dryer, mesh Wi-Fi with battery backup, a covered terrace and your own private dipping pool — with the resort-style shared pools a few steps away.
