# Natal photo shot list — next Tulum trip

## Tips
- Daytime, curtains open, ALL lights on (incl. under-cabinet + pool lights)
- Camera at chest height, landscape orientation, level horizon
- Avoid the widest lens setting — it bends walls; step back instead
- Tidy first: cables, cleaning supplies, bags, product tags off the bed frames
- Take 3–4 frames of each, vary angle slightly
